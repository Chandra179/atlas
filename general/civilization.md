1. Mesopotamia / Ancient Near East — from roughly 3500 BCE
   Book: The Ancient Near East: History, Society and Economy — Mario Liverani
   Start here for Sumer, Akkad, Babylon, Assyria, early cities, writing, kingship, trade, and warfare. Sumerian urban civilization is among the earliest known.
2. Ancient Egypt — from roughly 3100 BCE
   Book: The Oxford History of Ancient Egypt — edited by Ian Shaw
   Covers the unification of Egypt, pharaohs, pyramids, religion, government, and later Egyptian history.
3. Ancient India / South Asia — from roughly 2600 BCE
   Book: A History of Ancient and Early Medieval India — Upinder Singh
   Begins with earlier archaeology and then covers the Indus Valley Civilization, Vedic societies, Mauryan Empire, Buddhism, and later Indian kingdoms.
   Then: The Archaeology of South Asia — Robin Coningham & Ruth Young, if you want more archaeological detail.
4. Early China — prehistoric periods through Shang and Zhou
   Book: The Archaeology of China: From the Late Paleolithic to the Early Bronze Age — Li Liu & Xingcan Chen
   This actually reaches farther back into prehistory than most books on this list, so you could even read it first if you want to begin with human settlement rather than civilization. It explains agriculture, Neolithic cultures, early cities, and the development of Chinese states.
   Then: The Cambridge History of Ancient China — edited by Michael Loewe & Edward L. Shaughnessy, especially for the Shang and Zhou periods, roughly 1600–221 BCE.
5. The Maya — roots from around 2000 BCE; major cities later
   Book: The Maya — Michael D. Coe & Stephen Houston
   Covers early Maya society through the great Classic cities, writing, astronomy, kingship, religion, warfare, and the post-Classic period.
6. Ancient Southeast Asia — prehistoric societies to early kingdoms
   Book: Southeast Asia: From Prehistory to History — edited by Ian Glover & Peter Bellwood
   Useful for understanding how agriculture, maritime trade, metallurgy, and early states developed in areas including what are now Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Cambodia, Malaysia, and neighboring regions.
7. Ancient Korea — early states and Three Kingdoms
   Book: A History of Korea — Kyung Moon Hwang
   Its ancient sections cover early Korean societies and kingdoms including Goguryeo, Baekje, and Silla.
8. Ancient Japan — especially state formation from the first millennium CE
   Book: The Cambridge History of Japan, Volume 1: Ancient Japan
   Covers early Japanese society, the emergence of the Yamato state, Buddhism, imperial government, and relations with China and Korea.
9. The Aztecs / Mexica — approximately 1300s–1521 CE
   Book: The Aztecs — Michael E. Smith
   Read this last chronologically. The Aztec Empire was much younger than Egypt, Mesopotamia, China, or the Maya. It covers Tenochtitlan, warfare, markets, tribute, agriculture, religion, and the Spanish conquest.
