# Assets and Resources

## Purpose

A curated directory of trusted websites, marketplaces, tools, and asset libraries for developing games with Godot.

Use this document to find and evaluate:

- 3D character models and environments
- Rigged characters and animation packs
- Weapons, props, and environment assets
- Textures, materials, and shaders
- 2D graphics, sprites, and UI assets
- Sound effects and music
- Fonts, icons, and UI resources
- Godot plugins, tools, and code examples
- Free, open-source, and paid development tools

## 1. Official Godot Resources

### Godot Asset Store
- Website: https://store.godotengine.org/
- Purpose: Discover Godot assets, plugins, and extensions.
- Source type: Official Godot Foundation store.
- Notes: Check each asset's version compatibility, documentation, and license.

### Godot Asset Library
- Website: https://godotengine.org/asset-library/
- Purpose: Find community-created Godot addons, scripts, tools, demos, and templates.
- Source type: Official Godot resource directory.
- Notes: The newer Godot Asset Store is replacing the older Asset Library. Check the current status of each service before choosing where to search.

### Godot Documentation
- Website: https://docs.godotengine.org/
- Purpose: Learn engine features, asset importing, scripting, physics, animation, and rendering.
- Source type: Official documentation.

### Godot License Information
- Website: https://godotengine.org/license/
- Purpose: Understand the Godot Engine license and distribution requirements.

## 2. Free and Open Asset Resources

### Kenney
- Website: https://kenney.nl/
- Purpose: Game-ready models, props, UI, environments, and prototype assets.
- Cost: Many assets are free.
- License: Kenney's game assets are generally CC0, according to its official support page. Verify the license included with the specific asset.
- Best for: Greyboxing, prototypes, UI, and placeholder assets.

### OpenGameArt
- Website: https://opengameart.org/
- Purpose: Community-created sprites, textures, models, music, and sound effects.
- Cost: Free downloads are available.
- License: Varies by asset. Check the individual license and attribution requirements.

### Poly Haven
- Website: https://polyhaven.com/
- Purpose: HDRIs, textures, and 3D models.
- Cost: Free.
- License: Assets are published under CC0; verify the details for the asset being downloaded.
- Best for: Environment lighting, realistic surfaces, and environment props.

### ambientCG
- Website: https://ambientcg.com/
- Purpose: PBR materials, textures, and HDRIs.
- Cost: Free.
- License: Check the site's current license information.
- Best for: Stone floors, dirt, wood, metal, and arena surfaces.

## 3. 3D Models, Characters, and Animation

### Sketchfab
- Website: https://sketchfab.com/
- Purpose: Discover downloadable 3D models, including characters, weapons, and environments.
- Cost: Free and paid offerings may be available.
- License: Varies by model. Check download availability, commercial permissions, attribution, and modification restrictions.

### Mixamo
- Website: https://www.mixamo.com/
- Purpose: Character rigging and animation resources.
- Cost: Availability depends on the service's current terms.
- Best for: Learning animation workflows and prototyping humanoid movement.
- Notes: Verify character compatibility, animation licensing, and retargeting requirements before use.

### Blender
- Website: https://www.blender.org/
- Purpose: Create and edit 3D models, rigs, animations, UV maps, and materials.
- Cost: Free and open-source software.
- Best for: Creating or correcting the samurai model, skeleton, weapon grip, and combat animations.

## 4. 2D Graphics and Pixel Art

### Aseprite
- Website: https://www.aseprite.org/
- Purpose: Create and animate pixel art, sprites, and sprite sheets.
- Cost: Paid software; check the official website for current options.
- Best for: Pixel-art UI, icons, and 2D assets.

### LibreSprite
- Website: https://libresprite.github.io/
- Purpose: Open-source pixel-art creation and animation.
- Cost: Free.
- Best for: Pixel-art workflows without purchasing Aseprite.

### itch.io
- Website: https://itch.io/game-assets
- Purpose: Browse game assets, asset bundles, tools, and sound packs.
- Cost: Free and paid offerings.
- License: Depends on the creator and individual product.
- Best for: Indie-game assets and stylized resources.

### Fab
- Website: https://www.fab.com/
- Purpose: Browse 3D models, materials, environments, animations, and other digital assets.
- Cost: Free and paid offerings.

### Unity Asset Store
- Website: https://assetstore.unity.com/

## 7. License and Trust Checklist

Before using any asset, verify:

- [ ] The asset comes from the creator or a reputable distribution platform.
- [ ] The license permits the intended use.
- [ ] Commercial use is allowed if the game may be sold.
- [ ] Attribution requirements are understood and recorded.
- [ ] Modification and redistribution rules are understood.
- [ ] The asset is compatible with the project's Godot version and workflow.
- [ ] The download includes the expected files and documentation.
- [ ] The asset does not contain suspicious executables or unrelated files.
- [ ] The asset's source, license, and version are recorded in the project.

## 9. Asset Selection Rules

- Prefer official creator websites and reputable distribution platforms.
- Prefer open licenses when the terms suit the project.
- Do not assume that free downloads allow commercial use.
- Do not assume that an asset available on a marketplace is compatible with Godot.
- Avoid purchasing assets before checking previews, documentation, formats, and license terms.
- Keep downloaded assets and their license files organized.
- Record the source and license before integrating an asset.
- Use placeholder assets during prototyping and replace them only when necessary.
