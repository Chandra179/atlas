# Timers and Tweens

## Timers

- [Fundamental] `Timer` nodes: one-shot and repeating timers
- [Fundamental] `await get_tree().create_timer(seconds).timeout` for short delays
- [Fundamental] Attack windows, cooldowns, and stagger durations
- [Improvement] Pausing timers with the scene tree

## Tweens

- [Fundamental] `create_tween()` and `tween_property()` for smooth value changes
- [Fundamental] Easing and transition types
- [Improvement] Chaining, parallel tweens, and killing tweens when objects are freed
- [Advanced] Tween-driven animation timing that respects combat phases
