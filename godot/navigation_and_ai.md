# Navigation and AI

## Navigation

- [Fundamental] `NavigationRegion3D` and navigation meshes for walkable areas
- [Fundamental] `NavigationAgent3D` for moving toward a target
- [Improvement] Avoidance between agents
- [Advanced] Dynamic navigation mesh updates for changing arenas

## AI Opponent

- [Improvement] Simple AI controller that shares the player's character interface (see [character.md](character.md))
- [Improvement] State-based behavior: approach, attack, block, retreat
- [Improvement] Reaction timing and difficulty levels
- [Advanced] Behavior trees or utility-based decisions
